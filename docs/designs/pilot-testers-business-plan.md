# Design: Pilot testers on a free 2-month Business plan

## Problem Statement
The design-partner accountants' clients should use MTB3A for two months with every feature open (nothing blocked, limits only), see what each plan costs and what they are limited to, be onboarded properly, and see that they pay nothing now. Only the operator can extend. In return the founder wants to learn if the product is usable and intuitive, how easy it is to scan a receipt and issue an invoice from phone and from web, and which extra features, if any, they want.

## Demand Evidence
None yet for the pilot itself. The demand signal is upstream: accountants are working as design partners and want their clients on the product (accountant-channel design). No tester has been watched using it.

## Status Quo
Operator-issued accounts get a manual `billing:grant` (tier, reason up to 200 characters, optional expiry). The existing pilot (docs/commercial/pilot-offer.md, runbook) is for 10 paying users at founder prices, 3 months. Nothing shows a granted person a pilot, an end date, prices or limits.

## Target User & Narrowest Wedge
Target: a client of a design-partner accountant (a freelancer or small business owner). Named individuals do not exist yet; the accountant picks them (open item, see Assignment).
Wedge: the smallest version is the grant that already exists. Business, 60 days, issued today by CLI, with the founder watching the first sessions. Everything else (Pilot panel, cohort view, reminders) is built while those sessions run.

## Constraints
- "Full plan" means Business: all features, 3 businesses, 2 partners, 250 documents and 500 scans a month. Not Firm, no new tier.
- Limits are counted, not refused, on paid plans (MUT-735, `usage/caps.ts`; only Starter refuses at its cap), so testers see usage but hit no wall.
- A grant outranks the trial and Stripe (entitlement rule 2), so a tester who also has a trial is covered by the grant.
- Prices: the code holds none for Solo, Business or Firm (MUT-702 Part B held for MUT-700, who sells). The prices block ships only with final prices; until then Billing shows "prices open soon" (existing billingView state).
- Extension is operator-only by construction: a tester cannot create or edit a grant.
- Release-only telemetry (ADR-236) and Arabic-first RTL apply to every new surface; web and phone are designed together.

## Premises
1. "Full plan" = Business tier, delivered as the existing operator grant, 60 days. Agreed.
2. The product work is the tester's view: a Pilot state on Billing (web and phone) with "Free until <date>", the three plans (prices marked "Expected" until final), own limits with usage, and first-run onboarding. Agreed; copy and layout are in the Design Review section (minimal copy, no explanatory lines).
3. A pilot is a cohort: each grant is tagged with the pilot and the referring accountant; the operator console lists the cohort and end dates; reminders go out before the end. Agreed.
4. Learning comes from watching 5 to 8 testers do a first scan and a first invoice unaided, plus recorded milestones; feature wishes are asked after 3 to 4 weeks. Agreed.
5. The paying-pilot offer and runbook stay; a free, 2-month, feedback-first cohort section is added beside them with its own end rule. Agreed.

## Approaches Considered
- A, grant only: rejected as the whole answer (testers see nothing in the product), kept as slice 0.
- C, self-redeem link per accountant: rejected for now. Self-signup is off in production and it opens an abuse surface for a cohort of 5 to 8.
- B, pilot as a first-class state: chosen.

## Recommended Approach
B, built in slices so watched sessions start in days, not weeks.

- **S0 (today, no code):** grant Business for 60 days with the existing CLI (`npm run billing:grant -- --email … --tier BUSINESS --reason "pilot <accountant>" --expires …`). Start watching.
- **S1 server:** pilot marker on the grant (cohort name, referring accountant, expiry required, 60-day default; one Prisma migration, warn the orchestrator before merging). `GET /v1/billing` returns `pilot { cohort, endsAt }` for a pilot grant. Operator console: pilot cohort list with end dates and a bulk grant. Reminder mails: deferred (eng review D3).
- **S2 web:** Pilot panel in the Billing drawer (reuses usage lines and the "open soon" price state) and a first-run onboarding tour; ar + en.
- **S3 phone (Flutter):** the same Pilot state and onboarding.
- **S4 docs:** amend pilot-offer and runbook with the free cohort (what testers are promised, how to grant, how to extend, how it ends).
- **S5 milestones:** record first scan, first invoice and last step reached per tester, shown in Operator › Usage, so each watched session can be compared with what the product recorded.

End-of-pilot rule (to confirm): when the grant expires the person falls through the normal order. With `STARTER_PLAN` on they continue free on Starter (nothing locked, ADR-284); with it off they are read-only and can download their records. Either way they keep their data. Extending is a new operator grant; the panel says "write to us".

## Open Questions
1. Prices: do final Solo, Business and Firm prices exist in time, or does the panel say "open soon" at launch? Depends on MUT-700.
2. Is `STARTER_PLAN` on when the first pilot ends? It decides whether testers land on Starter or read-only.
3. Who creates the tester's account: the accountant on their behalf (accountant-channel delegated onboarding) or the operator? The grant is issued after the account exists.
4. 60 days exactly, or "2 calendar months" from the grant date?
5. Do testers consent to being observed or recorded in the watched sessions, and is screen recording allowed (PostHog session replay stays off unless decided)?

## Success Criteria
- 5 to 8 testers watched doing a first scan and a first invoice, with no help, on both phone and web.
- For each: time to first scan, time to first invoice, where they stopped, one thing that surprised the founder.
- A written list of friction points ranked by how many testers hit them, and a feature-wish list collected only after week 3 to 4.
- At day 50, each tester answers which plan they would pay for, if any.
- Every tester sees their end date and limits in the product; no tester is blocked by a limit during the pilot.

## QA scenarios (release-qa)
- An operator grants a pilot Business plan; the tester signs in and sees the Pilot panel with the end date, the plans and their usage.
- A tester scans a receipt and issues an invoice on a pilot account with nothing blocked.
- A pilot grant expires: the person lands on Starter or read-only as configured, and the records are still downloadable.
- The operator extends a pilot; the panel shows the new end date.

## Dependencies
MUT-700 (seller and final prices), MUT-731 (Starter plan switch), accountant-channel design (delegated onboarding), the paying-pilot runbook (amended, not replaced). Prisma migration in S1.

## The Assignment
This week, before any code: take the design-partner accountant's first customer from the "10 customers" list, issue the S0 grant, and sit behind them (or on a call, screen shared) while they scan one receipt and issue one invoice on their phone, then one on the web. Say nothing. Write down the first place they hesitate. Bring that sentence back before S2's onboarding is designed, because it is the first thing the onboarding has to fix.

## What I noticed about how you think
- You asked for "nothing blocked" and "limits of course" in the same breath. You want a real trial of the product, with the commercial shape visible.
- You said "this will be the first time" about watching a user. Most founders hide that; saying it is what makes the pilot worth running.
- You put "can be extended only by operator" in the first message. Control over the end of the pilot came before the start.

### Responsive and accessibility
- 375 px: one column, plans below limits, no pinned bar; every action at least 44 px; the drawer fills the screen (existing rule); logical properties only, so Arabic mirrors.
- A line's number is always shown with its tone, so colour never carries the meaning alone; each usage line reads "<name>, <used> of <cap>" to a screen reader; the chip is words, not colour.
- Counts in the mono numerals; dates in the person's language (Levantine month names in Arabic).

### Architecture
```
operator ──billing:grant (tier BUSINESS, expiry, cohort, accountant)──> entitlement_grants (+ cohort, referredBy)
tester ──GET /v1/billing──> route: entitlements + usage (existing)
                            + pilotOf(grants, entitlement source, now) ──> pilot { cohort, endsAt, plans[expected prices] }
                                                     ^ pilot-prices.json
Welcome closed ──POST (once)──> users.pilotWelcomeSeenAt
web/phone ──PostHog (release-only gate)── scan, invoice, plans viewed, link tapped (tag pilot)
operator Accounts list ──?pilot=1──> rows + chip (cohort, endsAt) + ticks (one batched usage read)
```
`pilotOf` returns a pilot only when the person's covering entitlement source is OPERATOR, the grant is not ACCOUNTANT, not revoked and not expired at the same `now` the entitlement used; with several live pilot grants it takes the latest expiry.

### Tests (every new behaviour)
- `pilotOf` unit: live; expired; revoked; ACCOUNTANT ignored; two grants (latest expiry); person also holds a paying subscription (no pilot).
- Billing route: `pilot` present for a pilot grant, absent for a plain grant, trial, Stripe; `plans` from the JSON; OpenAPI check.
- Store contract (memory and Postgres): grant create with cohort and accountant; user welcome flag set once and idempotent; operator list `pilot=1` with cursor continuation; migration test for the two additive nullable columns.
- Web: `billingView` pilot kind in every state; usage line butter at 90% and not at 89%; Welcome opens once per account and not after closing; no explanatory strings (copy guard); ar and en keys equal.
- Phone parity tests in the Flutter repo.
- Release-qa: the four scenarios in this doc, run once on web.

### Failure modes
| Mode | Handled by |
|---|---|
| Grant expires between two reads | One `now` passed to both the entitlement and `pilotOf` |
| Tester subscribes during the pilot | Pilot hidden once the covering source is STRIPE |
| Extension (revoke + new grant) | Welcome flag is on the user, so it does not repeat |
| Prices file missing or malformed | `pilot.plans` empty; the Plans list shows names and limits without a price; boot check in tests |
| Migration | Two additive nullable columns; warn the orchestrator before merging; API minor bump; regenerate OpenAPI and portal types |

Reminder mails at day 45 and 55 (D3); server-side PostHog; bulk "Add testers" UI; a grant update action (extension stays revoke + new grant).
