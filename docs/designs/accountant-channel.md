# Design: Accountant channel, delegated onboarding and write access

Generated by /office-hours on 2026-11-26
Branch: main
Repo: mini-crm (MTB3A)
Status: APPROVED
Mode: Startup
Supersedes: basel-main-design-20261026-111558.md (earlier MTB3A design; this is a new direction, not a revision)

## Problem Statement
MTB3A started as expense recording for freelancers and small firms and now issues invoices. Accountants want it for their own work, and they are the natural sales channel: an accountant can sign up his customers, activate their subscription in front of them, and keep working in their books. Today an accountant can only read one shared profile through an owner's invite (ADR-245, read-only), and only sees expenses that have a receipt (ADR-258).

## Demand Evidence
- A named accountant, now a design partner, helps develop the system. He has **400+ customers** and said he will onboard all of them once the system is "fully functional".
- Not yet evidence: that sentence is a conditional, not a commitment. No customer is activated, no accountant has paid, and "fully functional" is not written down.

## Status Quo
For customers who are not tech-savvy he issues their invoices himself. For others he sends them to a similar service (Invoice4U, iCount) to make his own life easier. So the accountant is the day-to-day operator today.

## Target User & Narrowest Wedge
- User: the design-partner accountant (400+ customers), and later other accountants like him.
- Wedge (A): he creates a customer's profile, activates its subscription in front of them, and issues their invoices, with the customer claiming the account later.
- Per customer, both modes exist (delegated or self-serve). The first release picks delegated as the default because his customers do not self-serve.

## Constraints
- Reverses or narrows recorded rules: ADR-037 and ADR-088 (nobody provisions into another's account, the person sets their own password through a setup link), ADR-245 (accountant is read-only, never a member), ADR-251 (accountant plan is a capacity for reading).
- Accountants never pay for their customers. The customer's profile carries its own subscription; the accountant has his own plan for his own use.
- Card data never touches MTB3A servers (Stripe-hosted fields only).
- Telemetry is release-only (ADR-236). All text through i18n, Arabic first.

## Premises
1. First goal is activated customers, not features; the design partner's written definition of "functional" is the launch gate. (Agreed)
2. The accountant creates the customer's account and profile; the customer claims it through a one-time setup link (ADR-088), sets a password, gets the passkey nudge. The accountant never learns a password. (Agreed)
3. The accountant who creates a profile has delegated write access to it from creation; consent is shown and recorded at claim; either side can revoke; every action is audited as "done by accountant". Write access is limited to one customer's books. (Agreed, with the change that access exists from creation)
4. Card: when the customer is present, the accountant types the card into a Stripe-hosted field on his device, the customer approves the bank's 3-D Secure check there, and the subscription activates before any password exists. When the customer is absent, a payment link. (Corrected in session: customer-present is a standard customer-initiated payment, not MOTO.)
5. Three role layers: platform (operator, accountant, business user), per-profile (owner, delegate accountant, read-only accountant), and the accountant's own plan. (Mostly agreed; accountant plan contents still open.)

## Research Notes (untrusted web content, used as data)
- Stripe MOTO needs enablement by Stripe support and a PCI check, is for cardholder-not-present, and I found no source confirming it works for Subscriptions renewals. It only matters if an accountant keys a card for a customer who is not there, which this design avoids.
- FreshBooks: an accountant who creates a client account gets a full-access role; a standard role cannot see Clients, Projects or billing. A precedent for two accountant roles.

## Approaches Considered
- **A: Delegated write grant on the existing model (chosen).** Add a write level to `accountant_grants`; one call creates the passwordless user, the profile and an ACTIVE write grant; setup link to the customer; in-person Stripe activation. Reuses grants, `selectProfile`, ADR-088 links, ADR-160 billing, the MUT-562 desk, MUT-418 trial start. Effort L (human ~4-6 weeks / CC ~2-3 days). Risk medium.
- B: Accountant practice as its own entity (staff, bulk import, practice billing). Rejected for now: XL, touches every membership reader that ADR-245 avoided, builds before one customer is activated. Revisit after the partner shows what a practice needs.
- C: Accountant-held profile transferred on claim. Rejected: two owners over time, unclear owner of the card before claim, deeper reversal of ADR-037.

## Recommended Approach
A. Wireframes: none yet (desktop portal and Flutter phone as interactive HTML, per Basel's rule, go to /plan-design-review).

Sketch of the work, to be confirmed by plan review:
1. Server: grant `level` READ|WRITE; write routes are a deny-by-default allowlist (not derived like the read allowlist); audit actor field "acting accountant"; `POST /v1/accountant/customers` (create user + profile + grant + setup link in one transaction); revoke by either side.
2. Billing: Stripe SetupIntent/Checkout started by the accountant for the customer's profile; present flow on the accountant's device with 3-D Secure; absent flow by link; subscription active at once; trial rules from MUT-418.
3. Claim: setup link lands on password + passkey nudge (existing), with the consent line for the accountant's access.
4. Portal desk (MUT-562) gets "Add customer", the customer list with state (created, subscribed, claimed), and "work in this customer's books".
5. Roles: write down the three layers in DECISIONS.md with an override log for ADR-037, ADR-088, ADR-245, ADR-251.
6. Dark launch behind a switch, like ACCOUNTANT_ACCESS.

## Scope amendment (2026-11-26, /plan-ceo-review, Basel's instruction on D12)
Supersedes Premise 3, Approach A's write grant, the "accountant operates" wedge and Recommended Approach items 1 and 2 above.
- The accountant **sets up** the customer's profile and helps them get started. He does **not** act on their behalf at this point.
- His access is the existing accountant access (ADR-245, ADR-258): view issued invoices and scanned receipts. It is created ACTIVE at provisioning. No write level, no acting-as path, no 2FA-for-writes build (ACCT-1, ACCT-5, ACCT-8 superseded).
- Kept as approved: provisioning with a passwordless account and setup link (ADR-088), the claim-safety code and change-email (ACCT-6), the operator-approved accountant flag and per-accountant daily limit (ACCT-2, ACCT-7), card first on a paid plan by the accountant in person or the customer (ACCT-4), no accountant limits now with the plan redesign filed as MUT-747 (ACCT-3), accountant never pays.
- Effort drops: no change to the three read-only guards of ADR-245; the new work is the provision route, the activation route, the claim step and the desk screens.
- Open Questions 3 to 5 lose most of their weight: no agency liability for acting, no accountant Tax Authority credentials, no delegate write scope.

## Open Questions
1. What exactly does the partner mean by "fully functional"? Get it in writing, as a list.
2. Accountant plan: what does his own plan include, and how is it priced relative to the free tier of 3 clients (ADR-251)?
3. Liability and consent: is the accountant acting as the customer's agent? Terms for the customer at claim; what the accountant signs. Needs a legal read (Barmajiyat Ltd).
4. Tax Authority allocation numbers (MUT-736): does the accountant's invoice go out under the customer's connection? Which credentials, and who holds them?
5. Which of expenses, invoices, receipts scanning may a delegate write? (ADR-258's receipt-only rule is for read-only accountants and does not obviously apply.)
6. What happens to an activated subscription whose customer never claims the account? Reminder and expiry rules.
7. Bulk import of existing customers: out of scope for A, but his 400 will ask for it.
8. If a card is keyed for a customer who is absent: allowed or link only? (MOTO is unverified for subscriptions.)

## Success Criteria

- Zero card data in logs or databases; the audit log shows who did what for every delegated write.
- Of 10, at least 7 claim the account (set a password), and the passkey nudge is shown to all.
