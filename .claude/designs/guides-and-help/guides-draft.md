# Draft guide steps (for the art; not signed off)

Plain, minimal copy. Arabic is written first in the product and arrives with the signed-off copy deck (MUT-673); the Arabic titles are given here so the right-to-left context is clear. Steps are checked against the real screens when each guide is built.

## 1. Connect to the Tax Authority (الربط بمصلحة الضرائب)

You let MTB3A act for your business to get allocation numbers.

1. Open Connect in Settings › Invoicing.
2. Sign in to the Tax Authority personal area with your own account.
3. Approve access.
4. Back in MTB3A you see "Connected until" and a date.

Note: reconnect every 90 days. Link: the Tax Authority's own page.

## 2. Connect your own Claude (ربط Claude)

Shown only to accounts that have it.

1. Open Connect Claude under You.
2. In your assistant, add MTB3A as a connector.
3. Approve on MTB3A's page.
4. Ask it to log a receipt. It asks where to file: a profile, or your inbox.

Note: MTB3A itself sends nothing to an AI.

## 3. Scan a receipt (مسح إيصال), phone

1. Open Scan from Add.
2. Point at the receipt. It is read.
3. Check the fields and file it.

## 4. Share a bank text (مشاركة رسالة البنك), phone

1. Open the bank's message.
2. Share it to MTB3A.
3. The expense is prepared. Check it and save.

## 5. Invite your accountant (دعوة المحاسب)

1. Invite them by email.
2. They accept.
3. They can look at entries and receipts, not change them.
