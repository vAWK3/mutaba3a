MUT-260: portal Receipts, three layouts

Each layout covers all four jobs: list a profile's receipts with a To review filter, open the original, upload a receipt without an expense, and review it. Reviewing opens the expense form beside the receipt, with the phone's suggested fields prefilled, and Approve files it. The vendor typeahead (MUT-254) sits in that form in every layout. The green fields came from the phone's reader.

A · Receipts in the sidebar: a table, and review in a wide drawer

A new Receipts nav item after Expenses. A table like Expenses, with To review / Filed / All and a count. Review opens the existing ExpenseDrawer widened to two panes: the receipt on the left, the form on the right.
