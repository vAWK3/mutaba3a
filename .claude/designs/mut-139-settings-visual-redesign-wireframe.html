Settings — visual redesign proposal

Same IA already shipped in MUT-139 (order, merges, restored toggle). This layer is purely look-and-feel: materials, hierarchy, grouping and motion, judged against the Apple-design principles — Grouping & Mapping, Simplicity (common path first), Materials/depth, and critically-damped motion for state switches. Nothing here is implemented in the app yet; this is the thing to approve before it is.

Profiles — promotedLarger title, avatar chips. It's the identity anchor; nothing else on the page means anything without one.
Preferences — one card, not twoLanguage, Theme and Currency are all quick, low-stakes defaults. One header and one card instead of three — the "Currency" label inside is plain text, not a heading (the real a11y test forbids h3s).
Advanced Features — marked, not alarmedAn icon signals "this changes your nav," not a danger color — it's structural, not destructive.
Data + Data Tools — nestedData Tools becomes a quieter sub-card inside Data's card, reading as one family instead of two siblings.
About — de-emphasizedSmallest type, no card chrome, bottom of the page. The least load-bearing content gets the least visual weight.
Segmented controlsLanguage/Theme/Currency toggles get a sliding-thumb segmented control instead of discrete buttons — one control, not N buttons that happen to look related.
No FX rates yetManual FX rate entry (CLAUDE.md spec, "All converted" reports) stays unbuilt — ADR-063 scope note, not shown in the card copy itself.
