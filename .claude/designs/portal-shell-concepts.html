The island opens your page.

Variation 2's island stays as the way in, and everything behind it moves to a dedicated You page instead of a long menu. The one thing that shouldn't need a whole page is switching profiles, which people do often and quickly. These three variations differ in where that quick switch lives.

Same in all three

Four tabs in the pill. The island shows the profile's name and your avatar, ringed in the profile's colour.

The You page

A real route (/p/:id/you). It grows out of the island, which stays lit while you're there. Any tab, or Back, takes you out the way you came.

Menus stay short

The only menu is the profile list: three profiles and a "Manage" link. Everything else is a row on the page that opens today's drawers.

1

Island → page, switch above the figure

Press the island and the You page opens. To change books without leaving the page you're on, use the small Sader Studio ⌄ · October ⌄ line above every figure, which drops a short profiles-only menu.

Press the island, then a tab to leave. Then press "Sader Studio ⌄" above the figure.

Why

One door for "me", and the profile switch sits right next to the numbers it changes. The island is a single button with a single meaning.

Trade-off

The profile's name appears twice on screen (island and line), and the switch can't be found from a page without that line, such as a client detail page.

2

Split island: name switches, avatar opens

The island has two halves. ● Sader Studio ⌄ drops the short profiles menu, and the avatar opens the You page. The page itself carries no extra line: the island is the one place for both. On the phone, the name sits at the top and the avatar circle sits beside the tab bar.

Press the name half, then the avatar half. Switch profiles and watch the ring and name change.

Why

Switching is one tap from every screen, client details included. The page body stays clean, and the island answers both "which books?" and "who am I?" in one object.

Trade-off

Two targets in one capsule have to read as two. That needs a clear divider and the chevron on the name half.

3

Island → page in three short parts

The island opens the You page, but the page is split into Profiles · Account · App with a segmented control, so no part is ever long. It opens on Profiles, so switching books is one press on the island plus one on a profile. There's no extra menu anywhere.

Press the island, switch profile on the first part, then try Account and App.

Why

No menus at all, one way in, and each part fits on a phone screen without scrolling. Each part can be deep-linked (/you/account).

Trade-off

Switching profile takes two presses and a page change. Fine for monthly visitors, slower for firms that switch all day.

Nothing lost: where each thing goes

Every item from today's profile switcher, account menu and shell. Signed out on this device, the account part shrinks to Sign in, Your data and Terms (toggle at the top).

My lean: 2, the split island. It keeps the thing you do often (switch books) one tap away on every screen, including client details, and moves the thing you do rarely (account settings) to a calm page with room to grow. The page body never carries chrome. If two targets in one capsule test poorly, 1 is the fallback with the same You page.
